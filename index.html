K-Means Algorithm — Step by Step
1

Data Collection

Load retail/e-commerce customer records with income, spending & demographics

2

Preprocessing

Handle missing values, encode gender (0/1), normalize with Z-score standardization

3

Feature Selection

Annual Income & Spending Score chosen as primary 2D clustering features

4

Elbow Method

Plot WCSS vs K (2–11); pick the "elbow" where inertia rate-of-change flattens

5

Silhouette Score

Validate K by computing mean silhouette — higher is better (max = 1.0)

6

K-Means++

Smart centroid init: pick first at random, each next proportional to distance²

7

E-Step

Assign every customer to the nearest centroid using Euclidean distance

8

M-Step

Recompute centroids as mean of all assigned points; repeat until convergence

📐 Mathematics

WCSS (Within-Cluster Sum of Squares)

WCSS = Σᵢ Σₓ∈Cᵢ ‖x − μᵢ‖²

Minimize total squared distance from each point to its centroid

Silhouette Score

s(i) = (b(i) − a(i)) / max(a(i), b(i))

a(i)=mean intra-cluster dist, b(i)=mean nearest-cluster dist

Euclidean Distance (Assignment)

d(x,μ) = √( Σⱼ (xⱼ − μⱼ)² )

Straight-line distance in feature space

Centroid Update (M-Step)

μᵢ = (1/|Cᵢ|) Σₓ∈Cᵢ x

New centroid = mean position of all assigned points
